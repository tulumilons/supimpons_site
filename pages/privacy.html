Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle user information when you use the Supimpons mobile application (the "App") and website (supimpons.com).

Effective Date: October 31, 2026 Last Updated: October 31, 2026

01 Offline-First Philosophy & Data Storage

Supimpons is built as an offline-first application:

  • Local Device Storage: All flashcards, study progress, customized vocabulary, tags, and review statistics are stored locally on your device (using SQLite).
  • No Cloud Synchronization by Default: Your personalized learning content is not uploaded to our servers, keeping your learning data private and under your direct control.

02 Information We Collect

Because Supimpons works locally, we collect minimal to zero personal identification:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register with your name, phone number, or password to use the core features of the App.
  • Aggregated Technical & Performance Data: If enabled by your device/app store settings (e.g., Google Play Services), anonymous crash reports, app performance metrics, and device diagnostics may be collected to help diagnose bugs and improve app stability.
  • Support Inquiries: If you contact us directly via email (supimpons@gmail.com), we receive your email address and any details you provide solely to resolve your issue.

03 No Advertising & No Marketing Trackers

Supimpons is completely ad-free:

  • Zero In-App Ads: We do not serve third-party banner, interstitial, or rewarded advertisements in the App or on our website.
  • No Ad Tracking Networks: We do not integrate advertising SDKs, ad brokers, or marketing telemetry tools to harvest your device identifiers or profile your behavior for targeted ads.

04 Children’s Privacy

Supimpons does not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us immediately to remove it.

05 Data Retention & Deletion

Because your study data is stored locally on your device, deleting the App or clearing app data via your device storage settings permanently deletes your local data.

06 Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
